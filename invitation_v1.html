Four Chapters of Destiny

Every turn in the stream brought us closer to forever

Chapter 01

A Skybound Encounter

Floating high above rolling valleys and cascading waterfalls. Just like the clouds outside the window, quiet conversations turned into comfortable laughter, revealing a connection as endless as the open sky.

"We realized home isn't a place — it's the person sitting across from you."
Chapter 02

Fairytale Meadows & Spiral Dreams

Strolling hand-in-hand through lush hills where whimsical towers rose to meet the clouds. With every step and spontaneous joke, the world began to feel like our very own storybook.

"Heart-shaped balloons in the breeze, and butterflies in our hearts."
Chapter 03

The Chair of Perspective

At the Museum of Illusions, Sunidhi stood grand while Praveen happily sat tiny in the oversized chair! A playful reminder that no matter who seems in charge, life with each other is full of fun angles and unscripted joy.

"Perspective is everything — and in our eyes, we found perfection."
Chapter 04

The Heart Wall: An Inevitable Yes

Roles reversed and hearts aligned! Standing beside walls covered in drifting hearts, Praveen gestured to the woman who stole his heart, making the promise to stand by her side through every twist of reality.

"No illusions here — just genuine, unconditional love."
